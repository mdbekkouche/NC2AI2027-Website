Artificial Intelligence for Healthcare: Recent Advances and Clinical Applications

This keynote will highlight recent advances in Artificial Intelligence for healthcare, with particular attention to clinical applications, medical diagnosis, disease prediction, medical imaging, personalized medicine, and clinical decision-support systems. It will also address the opportunities and challenges associated with applying AI to improve healthcare services and patient outcomes.

Keynote Themes

AI for Cybersecurity

Artificial Intelligence approaches for cybersecurity, emerging cyber threats, intelligent defense mechanisms, threat detection, and the protection of digital infrastructures.

AI for Healthcare Applications

Recent advances in Artificial Intelligence for healthcare, including medical diagnosis, disease prediction, medical imaging, personalized medicine, clinical decision-support systems, and AI-driven approaches to improving patient care.
